How Rating Works

Unofficial rating system for Chelcy 1.12.2.

01

Raw Score

Course performance

Your PB is compared with the current #1 time. A score of 100 means the PB equals the #1 time.

Raw Score = 100 × #1 Time / PB

02

Difficulty

0–1 · Updated monthly

Difficulty measures how hard it is to produce a time close to #1 — not how difficult the course itself is. It combines #1 outlierness and top-field competition. The table is refreshed at the beginning of each month and then stays fixed.

Difficulty = 2 × Outlier × Competition / (Outlier + Competition)
03

Adjusted Score

Difficulty bonus

Difficulty adds up to a 10% bonus to the Raw Score.

Multiplier = 1 + 0.10 × Difficulty
Adjusted Score = Raw Score × Multiplier
04

Best 30

Highest adjusted scores

The 30 highest Adjusted Scores are used. Higher positions have more weight.

Performance Index P = Σ(Adjusted Score × Weight) / Σ(Weight)
Exact weight formula
\[L(i)=\frac{1}{1+e^{0.18(i-20)}}\]\[w_i=0.50+0.50\cdot\frac{L(i)-L(30)}{L(1)-L(30)}\]\[w_1=1.00,\qquad w_{30}=0.50\]

Notes

Difficulty update

The Difficulty table is updated at the beginning of each month and remains fixed until the next update.

Eligible courses

Limited-time athletics are excluded.

Catalog-constrained theoretical maximum

With the current catalog and Difficulty table, first place on all 30 highest-Difficulty courses gives a theoretical Rating of 1375.03.
